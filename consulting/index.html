What we do

Seven ways we help.

  • Defense Contract Opportunity Research

    Identify government manufacturing solicitations and subcontracting opportunities that fit your shop’s capabilities, capacity, and qualifications.

  • Government Bid and Proposal Support

    Work through solicitation requirements, organize quotation information, and prepare clearer bid documentation before you submit.

  • Government Contract Quality Systems

    Build practical inspection, calibration, traceability, corrective-action, and quality-control procedures around the requirements of your contract.

  • ISO 9001 Consulting

    Quality manuals, procedures, gap assessments, and internal audit support to prepare your shop for an accredited certification body’s audit.

  • DoD PIEE/WAWF Support

    Help with electronic invoicing, receiving reports, routing, submission problems, and the administrative steps between delivery and payment.

  • Manufacturing Operations Improvement

    Shop-floor experience and Theory of Constraints principles applied to find bottlenecks, improve flow, and cut unnecessary rework.

  • CMMC Readiness Coordination

    Organize applicable cybersecurity readiness requirements and coordinate the technical work with qualified cybersecurity professionals.

Who you work with

Experience from the shop and the shipyard.

Our founder is a U.S. military veteran and a retired Norfolk Naval Shipyard professional with machine-shop and shipyard leadership experience. Our approach starts with your equipment, processes, customers, people, and contractual obligations, and aims for support that fits your operation without unnecessary paperwork.

What we are, and what we are not.

  • Cendavex is an independent consulting business. It is not an ISO certification body and not a CMMC assessor.
  • We do not guarantee contract awards, certification, government acceptance, or payment. Outcomes depend on your qualifications, your work, and decisions made by others.
  • Services are subject to project scope and applicable requirements.
