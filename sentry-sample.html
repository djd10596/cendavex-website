SENTRY · sample report

What a shop gets
every morning.

SENTRY reads open government solicitations for a machine shop and says which ones to bid, which to check, and which to pass, with the line in the solicitation that each reason comes from.

Where it stands today.

  • Two government feeds, the DLA DIBBS daily index and the SAM.gov contract opportunities extract, download on their own every night on our server, with a receipt for each run.
  • The report below was prepared on November 23, 2026 from real public DLA solicitations, with manual review. The fully automatic daily report is in build for a pilot with small shops starting December 1, 2026.
  • Public data only. Controlled drawings and technical data are never read or stored.
