SENTRY · sample report

Check our reading
against the notice.

SENTRY reads open government solicitations for a machine shop and says which to bid, which to check and which to pass, with the line in the solicitation each reason comes from. The pages below are from a sample made by hand on November 23, 2026 from real public DLA solicitations. Every solicitation number is listed, so you can look up the notice and judge the reading yourself.

Where it stands today.

  • Two government feeds, the DLA DIBBS daily index and the SAM.gov contract opportunities extract, download on their own every night on our server, with a receipt for each run.
  • A plain list of open DLA solicitations already runs every night with no one touching it. Reading each solicitation package and its past awards, the way this sample does, is the piece being built now. Until it is finished, every pilot list is checked by hand before a shop sees it.
  • Public data only. Controlled drawings and technical data are never read or stored.
  • A report shows the part only: NSN, name, quantity, dates and codes. It never shows or stores what the part goes on.

The three notices shown below.

All three were open when the sample was made. Their quote dates run from November 25 to November 30, 2026, so they are here to check our reading, not to quote.

  • SPE7L127T5645 · Shaft, shouldered · NSN 3040-01-269-9432 · 9 each · issued Nov 20, quotes due Nov 30, 2026 · AMSC L.
    Call: BID, check the drawing first. A turned part the shop can make. The drawing is not in the RFQ (it sits in DLA cFolders), so material, size and tolerances must be read before pricing. Page 3.
  • SPE7L127T4503 · Bracket, mounting · NSN 5340-00-695-9106 · 58 each · issued Nov 10, quotes due Nov 25, 2026 · AMSC G.
    Call: CHECK. The shop can make it, but the notice needs DLA approval for export-controlled data beyond JCP, and CMMC Level 2. If either is missing, it is a pass. Page 7.
  • SPE4A727T4743 · Pin, spring · NSN 5315-01-694-8753 · 2 each · issued Nov 20, quotes due Nov 27, 2026 · AMSC G.
    Call: CHECK. A standard NASM39086 pin to buy and resell, not machine. Past awards range from $4.76 each for 100 pieces (April 2026) and $7.76 each for 79 (January 2023) up to $410 for a single piece. Page 11.

Why 131 were passed before anything was read.

On the DIBBS index of November 20, 2026, 212 open DLA solicitations fell in the shop's six supply classes. Every DLA part carries a one-letter Acquisition Method Suffix Code (AMSC), set by DLA, that says how the part may be bought. SENTRY passed six codes, because a shop that is not already an approved source for the part cannot win it or has no usable data to make it from:

  • D (65): the data to make the part is not physically available.
  • R (31): rights to use the data are restricted.
  • C (16): buy only from an approved source.
  • Q (11): rights to the data would have to be purchased.
  • P (5): rights to use the data are legally unavailable.
  • B (3): source control.

That left 81 to read: AMSC G (56, data suitable and legally clear), L (23, low dollar value) and Z (2, commercial item). If your shop is already an approved source for a part, tell us and those solicitations come back into your list.
