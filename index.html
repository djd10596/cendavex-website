How It Works

One intelligence layer. Multiple markets. Your risk mandate.

Cendavex separates market intelligence from brokerage execution so analysis, risk controls, reporting, and strategy logic can evolve independently of any single broker.

Analyze

Find the Opportunity

Market scanners evaluate price action, volume, technical structure, sector strength, catalysts, and other relevant evidence.

Decide

Apply Intelligence

AI models evaluate candidates alongside deterministic trading rules and the trader's selected risk mandate.

Protect

Control the Risk

Position sizing, protective exits, exposure limits, and daily loss controls are applied before and during execution.

Execute

Use Supported Brokers

Broker adapters translate Cendavex instructions into the order capabilities supported by each integrated brokerage or venue.

Measure

Track the Evidence

Performance is measured against relevant benchmarks, with attention to drawdowns, consistency, risk level, and market conditions.

Learn

Continuously Improve

Cendavex is designed to use accumulated evidence to improve the platform while keeping risk controls explicit and testable.
