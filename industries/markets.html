Healthcare administration, business operations, software engineering, insurance administration, cybersecurity, energy operations and logistics are exploration areas. Applications such as workflow assistance, anomaly review and planning support remain hypotheses, not customer services.

Open questions

What evidence would show measurable benefit? Where must people retain authority? Which failure modes make an application unsuitable? These questions must be resolved before any offering.
