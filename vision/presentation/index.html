03 / SIX INDEPENDENT AGENTS

Six perspectives. One standard.

Plan / Build / Test / Secure / Verify / Review

Conceptual engineering choreography · Not a claim of deployed agents

Speaker notes

These six roles are a conceptual independent review architecture. Independence requires separate checks and evidence; this exhibit does not establish implementation.

04 / ENGINEERING DISCIPLINE

Confidence needs evidence.

Scoped permissions. Isolated changes. Tests. Human review.

Engineering principles · Verification before release

Speaker notes

Explain why tests, permission boundaries and human approval are necessary. These principles do not certify any particular system.

05 / FOUR DIVISIONS

One vision. Four horizons.

FINANCIAL / AI / SYSTEMS / INDUSTRIES

AI, Systems & Industries are prospective research divisions

Speaker notes

Introduce the four divisions. Explicitly distinguish the Financial beta from prospective research divisions.

06 / FINANCIAL

A proving ground for rigor.

Risk boundaries. Transparent decisions. Measurable behavior.

Founding beta · Paper money only · Live customer investing unavailable

Speaker notes

Financial provides a domain for examining risk and decision quality. The founding beta uses paper money; live customer investing is unavailable. No returns are promised.

07 / AI COMMERCIALIZATION

Useful intelligence.

Explore assistance for engineering, research and business.

Research concepts · No commercial AI products available

Speaker notes

Commercialization is conditional on validated utility, safety and demand. These are possibilities, not available products.

08 / SYSTEMS × INDUSTRIES

Future research · Feasibility, safety and demand must be demonstrated

Speaker notes

Discuss observability and supervised autonomy as research directions. Do not imply deployed infrastructure or industrial customers.
