Why I’m building Cendavex

I ran machine shops most of my life: job shop work and defense contracting. I still remember all the frustrations of trying to run a shop. Scheduling, making money, bidding defense work and commercial work at the same time.

Bidding government work was some of the hardest of it. Take the multi-year, multi-quantity contracts. You never know when they’re going to send an order, so you can’t plan for it. You can’t buy material in larger amounts for better pricing when you don’t know when the next order is coming.

And the specs can bite you. We had a medium-size job that required a certain coating. We didn’t understand the spec, but our painter said they did. They were wrong. The parts got rejected because the certs were wrong. Luckily the painter fixed it for free, because we did a lot of business with them. But that could have been really bad.

That’s why I’m building Cendavex: so a small shop knows what a solicitation really requires before it bids, not after the parts get rejected.

— Dan DiEmedio, Founder & CEO

About the founder

Dan DiEmedio is a U.S. Army veteran. He ran machine shops in the Philadelphia area, doing job shop work and defense contracting. In 2011 he moved to Hampton Roads to work at Norfolk Naval Shipyard, where he was Manager of the C931 machine shop for 3 years and then Zone Manager, C300, for 4 years before retiring. He has run machining on both sides of the contract: as a small shop bidding defense work, and inside the Navy’s own shipyard.

He founded Cendavex on Veterans Day, November 11, 2026, to build affordable, plain-English tools for small shops.

Cendavex is veteran-founded. We were born on Nov 11, 2026, commissioned on Nov 12 and went live on Nov 13. Our principle is simple: Trust, but verify. C200AI builds; C130AI independently verifies; the founder decides what matters.

“Transforming Industries” is our vision. We state it next to what has already happened.
