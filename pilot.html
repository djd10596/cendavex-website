Founding Shops pilot

Know what a job really
requires before you bid.

I’m looking for five machine shops to try SENTRY free. You tell me what your shop can do. I send you a short list of open DLA solicitations that fit, each marked bid, check or pass, with the reason and the line in the solicitation it comes from. I check every list myself before you get it.

Who can join.

  • A small U.S. machine shop.
  • Registered in SAM.gov with a CAGE code, or working on it. DLA can’t award to a shop without one.
  • Quotes DLA work on DIBBS, or wants to start.
  • Willing to mark each item on your list Quote or Pass, with a word on why.

JCP certification (DD Form 2345) helps, because many DLA drawings are released only to JCP-certified shops. It is not required to join; tell us on the form either way.

What you get.

  • A short list of open DLA solicitations that fit your shop, on weekdays during the pilot: which to bid, which to check and which to pass, with the line in the solicitation behind each reason.
  • Where a DLA solicitation prints its past awards, you see who won before, how many and at what unit price, before you quote.
  • Every list checked by me personally before it reaches you.

See a hand-checked sample with the solicitation numbers listed: SENTRY sample report.

How a list is picked.

  • Your supply classes and machines, from your profile.
  • DLA’s own purchase codes: parts only an approved source can win, or with no usable data to make them from, are passed. The sample page explains each code.
  • Machined parts only, ranked with small-business set-asides first, then the larger quantities.
  • Read for the things that change a price: drawing access, export control, CMMC, special processes, packaging and marking, inspection and first article.

So far: on the first two automatic lists (November 25 and 26, 2026), I marked all 17 top picks “Quote.” That is one machinist’s judgment on two days, not a measured accuracy rate. Measuring it with real shops is what the pilot is for.

Pilot terms.

  • Five shops, 60 days from your first list.
  • Free. No card, no contract, nothing to install, nothing billed when the pilot ends.
  • If SENTRY becomes a paid service, founding shops get the price in writing first and decide then.
  • Advice only: a help for your bid decision, not a promise. Check every point against the notice before you quote. You can leave at any time.

Your data.

  • Your profile is used only to build your list and to tune SENTRY’s rules from your Quote and Pass marks. It is never sold or shared.
  • Public information only. Please don’t send drawings, CUI, controlled technical data or export-controlled data. We’ll never ask for them.
  • Your list shows the part only: NSN, name, quantity, dates and codes. It never shows or stores what the part goes on.
  • Ask, and I delete your profile and your marks and confirm it to you in writing.

Your shop profile.

About 5 minutes, mostly clicks. If the form doesn’t show below, open it in a new tab.
